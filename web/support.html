⚠️ Reporting Inappropriate Content or Users

  • Select the reason for reporting
  • Our team reviews within 24 hours

  • Report Booking: Booking → ⋮ menu → Report

🗑️ Account Deletion Instructions

⚠️ Important: This action is permanent and cannot be undone.
  1. Open the Thumb Tech app
  2. Go to Profile (bottom navigation bar)
  3. Tap on Delete Account
  4. Read the confirmation dialog carefully
  5. Enter your password to confirm
  6. Tap Delete Permanently

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

📱 How do I book a service?
Open the app → Browse services → Select a service → Enter details → Confirm booking.
🔧 How do I become a technician?
Create an account → Select "Technician" role → Fill in your details → Submit for verification.
💳 Is my payment secure?
Yes! All payments are processed through Razorpay. We never store your credit card information.
🚫 How do I report a user?
Go to the user's profile → Tap the three dots (⋮) → Select "Report User" → Choose a reason → Submit.
🗑️ How do I delete my account?
Profile → Delete Account → Enter password → Confirm deletion. See instructions above.
