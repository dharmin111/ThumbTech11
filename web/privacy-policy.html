📋 1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

  • Name: To identify you and personalize your experience
  • Email Address: For account creation and communication
  • Phone Number: For service coordination and verification
  • Address: To locate service areas and dispatch technicians
  • Pincode: To match you with nearby service providers
  • Profile Picture: Optional - for identification purposes

1.2 Usage Data

  • Service Requests: Details of services you request or provide
  • Chat Messages: Communication between customers and technicians
  • Photos: Images uploaded for service requests (optional)
  • Device Information: Device type, OS version, and app usage analytics

1.3 Location Data

  • Approximate Location: Based on IP address or pincode for service matching
  • Exact Location: Only if you grant permission, for service delivery

1.4 Payment Information

  • We do not store or process credit card information directly
  • Payments are processed through Razorpay (secure third-party payment gateway)
  • We only store payment status and transaction IDs

☁️ 3. Data Storage

  • Firebase: All user data is securely stored on Google Firebase
  • Firebase Storage: Images and documents are stored in secure cloud storage
  • Data Security: Industry-standard encryption protocols
  • Data Retention: Data kept as long as your account is active
🔐 Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

🔗 4. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, Firestore database, Storage
  • OneSignal: Push notifications
  • Razorpay: Payment processing
  • Firebase Hosting: Web services and admin panel
ℹ️ These third-party services have their own privacy policies.

🤝 5. Data Sharing

We only share your information in the following cases:

  • With Service Providers: Technicians see only necessary information
  • With Payment Processors: Razorpay receives payment information
  • With Legal Authorities: If required by law
  • With Your Consent: We never sell your personal information
✅ We NEVER sell your personal information.

👤 6. Your Rights

  • Access: View all your data in the app profile
  • Edit: Update your information anytime
  • Delete: Request permanent deletion of your account
  • Opt-out: Disable notifications in settings
  • Data Portability: Request a copy of your data
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent anytime

  • We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13

  • 🔒 Data encrypted in transit (SSL/TLS)

  • 🔒 Limited access to personal data
